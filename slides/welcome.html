Framing

Perspectives on Composition in Robot Learning

  • Data availability dictates how structure is added to solve problems: language > video >>> vision–action.
  • Robotics is a rich playing ground for modularization and composition: a vast range of problem spaces (state, reward, actions, goals) and applications (long-horizon, grasping).
Questions for the day
  • How will scaling data in robotics influence modularity designs?
  • What should the interfaces between compositional or modular agents be?
  • Should compositionality (interpreting parts from the whole) be a goal in itself?
